Verification derivatives under `output/` replace the background using a model mask, apply an explicitly recorded manual background-corner brush stroke, resize and reframe the car onto an original studio background. Some later inspections add manual contact shadows and a solid plate rectangle. These derived fixture images are also CC BY-SA 4.0. Neither Kevauto nor Toyota endorses this software. The visible orange plate cover already appears in the original photograph.

Synthetic test fixtures are generated in tests with solid colors and simple brush masks; they are not represented as real vehicle AI results.
